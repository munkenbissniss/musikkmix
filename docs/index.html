Loops in, music video out.

musikkmix listens to an mp3 for tempo, structure, breaks and drops, studies your seamless video loops, and cuts them together on the beat. Built for big screens, runs locally on Ubuntu.

A simulated 64-beat track: intro, verse, break, drop, outro. Drag the slider to see how --intensity changes the cutting. Clips always start on the beat grid.

How it works

  1. Listen. Tempo, beats and bars, energy per beat, song sections (grouped, so the second chorus looks like the first), breaks, drops and build-ups.
  2. Watch. Motion over time, brightness, colour profile, and optionally CLIP embeddings for what is actually in the picture.
  3. Match. Each part of the song gets its own pool of loops that fit its energy. Start points inside each loop are chosen to match the music, and recently used footage is avoided.
  4. Cut. Calm parts get 8–16 beat shots, intense parts 1–2. Cuts come up faster before a drop, and the drop lands on the downbeat.
  5. Add motion. Optional crossfades in calm parts, plus zoom, shake and brightness pulses locked to beats, bars or kick drums.
  6. Render. ffmpeg builds a frame-accurate video at your resolution and adds the audio.

Everything runs on your machine. No uploads, no account.

Install

sudo apt install ffmpeg python3-venv
git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/musikkmix.git
cd musikkmix
python3 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
# optional AI matching (CLIP):
pip install torch transformers pillow

Use

./musikkmix.py track.mp3 loops/ -o mix.mp4
./musikkmix.py track.mp3 loops/ --resolution 3840x2160 --intensity 1.3 --drop-flash
./musikkmix.py track.mp3 loops/ --fx-preset punchy
./musikkmix.py track.mp3 loops/ --clip --theme "neon city at night"
./musikkmix.py track.mp3 loops/ --limit 40       # quick test of the first 40 s
./musikkmix.py track.mp3 loops/ --plan-only      # write the cut plan (JSON) only

Options

OptionWhat it does
--intensityHigher means faster cutting (0.5–2).
--min-cut-beats, --max-cut-beatsShortest and longest shot, in beats (1, 2, 4, 8, 16).
--randomness0.05 is nearly deterministic, 1 is wild.
--continuityPositive: similar shots follow each other. Negative: contrast.
--pool-sizeNumber of loops per song section group.
--clip, --themeUse CLIP for visual similarity, and favour loops matching a text prompt.
--fx-presetsubtle, punchy or wild: ready-made effect sets.
--crossfadeDissolve between shots in calm parts (seconds, max 0.5). Intense parts and drops keep hard cuts.
--zoom, --shake, --pulseZoom, shake and brightness pulses that decay after each trigger. Choose the trigger with --zoom-on, --shake-on, --pulse-on (beat, bar or kick), and --pulse-mode light|dark.
--drop-flashWhite flash on drops.
--audio-offsetShift all cuts by N seconds if they feel early or late.
--seedReproduce an earlier result. The seed is printed on every run.
--resolution, --fps, --crf, --jobsOutput size, frame rate, quality and parallelism.

Good to know

Beat tracking is not perfect. Music with a shifting tempo or very dense rhythms can confuse it, so check a short --limit render first and tweak --audio-offset or --seed. The “AI” here is signal analysis, clustering and optionally CLIP, not a language model. Rendering 4K with many loops needs temporary disk space.
